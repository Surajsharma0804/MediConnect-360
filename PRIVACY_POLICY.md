# Privacy Policy - MediConnect 360

**Effective Date**: December 13, 2025  
**Last Updated**: December 13, 2025

## 1. Introduction

MediConnect 360 ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal health information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our healthcare platform.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Personal Information
- **Identity Information**: Name, email address, phone number, date of birth
- **Authentication Information**: Password, two-factor authentication settings
- **Profile Information**: Profile picture, language preferences, timezone

### 2.2 Health Information (Protected Health Information - PHI)
- **Medical History**: Conditions, symptoms, treatments, medications
- **Vital Signs**: Blood pressure, heart rate, temperature, weight
- **Lab Results**: Blood tests, imaging studies, diagnostic reports
- **Appointment Data**: Scheduling, provider interactions, visit notes
- **Prescription Information**: Medications, dosages, pharmacy details
- **Insurance Information**: Coverage details, claims, billing information

### 2.3 Technical Information
- **Device Information**: IP address, browser type, operating system
- **Usage Data**: Pages visited, features used, time spent on platform
- **Location Data**: General location for provider matching (with consent)
- **Communication Data**: Messages with healthcare providers

### 2.4 Third-Party Information
- **OAuth Data**: Information from Google, GitHub when you sign in
- **Wearable Data**: Health data from connected devices (with consent)
- **Insurance Data**: Coverage verification from insurance providers

## 3. How We Use Your Information

### 3.1 Primary Healthcare Purposes
- **Treatment**: Facilitate medical care and provider communication
- **Payment**: Process insurance claims and billing
- **Healthcare Operations**: Quality improvement, care coordination

### 3.2 Platform Operations
- **Account Management**: Create and maintain your account
- **Communication**: Send appointment reminders, health alerts
- **Support**: Provide customer service and technical support
- **Security**: Detect fraud, prevent unauthorized access

### 3.3 Legal and Compliance
- **HIPAA Compliance**: Meet healthcare privacy requirements
- **GDPR Compliance**: Respect European privacy rights
- **Legal Obligations**: Comply with applicable laws and regulations

## 4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

### 4.1 Healthcare Providers
We share your health information with:
- **Your Healthcare Team**: Doctors, nurses, specialists you've authorized
- **Emergency Contacts**: In medical emergencies (as permitted by law)
- **Care Coordinators**: For treatment planning and follow-up

### 4.2 Business Associates
We may share information with:
- **Technology Vendors**: Cloud hosting, security services (under BAAs)
- **Payment Processors**: For billing and insurance processing
- **Analytics Providers**: For platform improvement (de-identified data only)

### 4.3 Legal Requirements
We may disclose information when:
- **Required by Law**: Court orders, subpoenas, regulatory requests
- **Public Health**: Disease reporting, safety investigations
- **Emergency Situations**: To prevent serious harm to you or others

### 4.4 We Do NOT Sell Your Information
- We never sell your personal or health information
- We don't share data for marketing purposes without explicit consent
- We don't provide data to data brokers or advertising networks

## 5. Data Security

### 5.1 Technical Safeguards
- **Encryption**: All data encrypted in transit and at rest
- **Access Controls**: Role-based access with multi-factor authentication
- **Monitoring**: 24/7 security monitoring and threat detection
- **Backups**: Secure, encrypted backups with disaster recovery

### 5.2 Administrative Safeguards
- **Staff Training**: Regular privacy and security training
- **Access Auditing**: Comprehensive audit logs of all data access
- **Incident Response**: Procedures for security breach response
- **Vendor Management**: Business Associate Agreements with all vendors

### 5.3 Physical Safeguards
- **Data Centers**: SOC 2 certified facilities with physical security
- **Device Security**: Encrypted devices, secure disposal procedures
- **Facility Access**: Restricted access to systems and data

## 6. Your Privacy Rights

### 6.1 HIPAA Rights
- **Access**: Request copies of your health information
- **Amendment**: Request corrections to your health information
- **Accounting**: Get a list of disclosures of your information
- **Restriction**: Request limits on use/disclosure of your information
- **Confidential Communication**: Request communication via specific methods

### 6.2 GDPR Rights (for EU residents)
- **Access**: Obtain confirmation of processing and copies of data
- **Rectification**: Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
- **Erasure**: Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
- **Portability**: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
- **Objection**: Object to processing based on legitimate interests
- **Restriction**: Limit processing in certain circumstances

### 6.3 How to Exercise Your Rights
- **Online**: Use your account settings to manage preferences
- **Email**: Contact privacy@mediconnect360.com
- **Phone**: Call our privacy hotline at [PHONE_NUMBER]
- **Mail**: Send written requests to our privacy officer

## 7. Data Retention

### 7.1 Health Information
- **Active Records**: Retained while you're an active user
- **Inactive Accounts**: Retained for 7 years after last activity (HIPAA requirement)
- **Legal Requirements**: May retain longer if required by law

### 7.2 Technical Data
- **Usage Logs**: Retained for 2 years for security and analytics
- **Audit Logs**: Retained for 7 years for compliance
- **Backup Data**: Retained for 1 year in encrypted backups

### 7.3 Account Deletion
- **User Request**: You can request account deletion at any time
- **Data Removal**: Most data deleted within 30 days
- **Legal Retention**: Some data may be retained for legal compliance

## 8. International Data Transfers

### 8.1 Data Location
- **Primary Storage**: United States (HIPAA-compliant data centers)
- **Backup Storage**: Encrypted backups in multiple geographic regions
- **Processing**: Data may be processed in countries where our service providers operate

### 8.2 Transfer Safeguards
- **Adequacy Decisions**: Transfers to countries with adequate protection
- **Standard Contractual Clauses**: For transfers to other countries
- **Binding Corporate Rules**: Internal data transfer policies

### 9.2 Analytics Cookies

### 9.3 Cookie Management
